Read the case study

The problem

My products, my background and my job search were spread across TikTok, Instagram, Selar and a few landing pages. Nobody could see them together.

Who it's for

Two groups: people deciding whether to buy one of my products, and recruiters or collaborators deciding whether to contact me.

How I organised it

The page opens with who I am and what I'm looking for, then my projects, my skills, and my products in their own dark section. That way each visitor finds their path without wading through the other.

Design decisions

The mahogany and cream palette comes from my brand colours. The product covers are drawn in CSS so they stay sharp on any screen. The only motion on load is the ring drawing around my photo, and it switches off for people who prefer reduced motion.

My part and the AI's part

I chose the content, the products, the colours and what to include. AI helped write the code, and I reviewed the result and asked for changes.

What I'd improve next

Add interior page previews for each product, give every project its own page, add a downloadable resume, and set up privacy-friendly analytics.
